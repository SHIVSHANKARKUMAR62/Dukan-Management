Privacy Policy

App: Dukan Management (dukanmg)

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Information We Store

The app allows shop owners to enter shop details, products, inventory, purchases, sales, customer details and other business records. The information stored depends on how you use the app.

2. Local Storage

Business information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own server to collect this information. Please note that device backups or other services may handle data separately if enabled.

3. Location

The app does not request the device's GPS location. Shop addresses can be entered manually by the user.

4. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally transmit locally stored business information to our own servers. If integrations or third-party services are added, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Data Security

Please keep your device secure and maintain backups of important business information. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can use the available deletion options in the app to remove supported locally stored data. Uninstalling the app may also remove local data. Export or back up information you need before deleting it.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for shop and business management, not specifically for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
